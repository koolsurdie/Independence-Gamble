# Reel script: "Busy is not growing"

**Platform:** Instagram Reels, 9:16 (1080x1920)
**Length:** about 60 seconds when read at a natural pace
**Goal:** viewers save the post and share it
**Main message:** keep reinventing yourself. Mindset comes first, learning comes next, and you measure it by impact, not by how busy you were.

---

## 1. HOOK (0–4s)
**Say:** "You took the course. You got the certificate. So why does nothing feel different?"
**On screen:** `BUSY ≠ GROWING` (the "≠" in amber)
**Shot:** close-up, looking straight at the camera. Start talking right away, with no "hi everyone".

## 2. WHY IT'S URGENT (4–12s)
**Say:** "Because the world isn't waiting for you. The skills our jobs need are changing faster than ever. The version of you that got you here won't get you there."
**On screen:** `The old you won't get you there.`
**Optional stat:** "The World Economic Forum expects around 39% of core job skills to change by 2030." *(Check this figure in the WEF Future of Jobs Report 2025 before you use it.)*

## 3. MINDSET (12–26s)
**Say:** "Reinvention starts in your head, not in your calendar. A learning mindset means three things. Curiosity: asking 'how does this work?' instead of 'that's not my job.' Open-mindedness: being willing to be wrong. And treating every failure as information, not a final judgement."
**On screen (one at a time):** `CURIOSITY` → `OPEN MIND` → `FAILURE = DATA`

## 4. ACTION (26–40s)
**Say:** "Then you act. Upskill: go deeper in what you already do. Reskill: learn something completely new. Go after that certification. Or build a project just for the experience. That is often where the real learning happens."
**On screen (one at a time):** `UPSKILL` → `RESKILL` → `CERTIFY` → `BUILD`

## 5. THE SHIFT: IMPACT, NOT ACTIVITY (40–54s)
**Say:** "But here's the shift. Don't count what you did. Count what changed. Not 'I finished a course', but 'I cut our reporting time in half.' Not 'I got certified', but 'I led the project because I could.' Activity is the input. Impact is the result."
**On screen:**
- `✗ I finished a course` → `✓ I cut reporting time by 50%`
- `✗ I got certified` → `✓ I led the project`
- Final card: `Count what CHANGED.` ("CHANGED" in amber)

## 6. CALL TO ACTION (54–60s)
**Say:** "Save this for the next time you feel stuck, and send it to someone who needs to hear it today."
**On screen:** `SAVE this 🔖  SHARE it ➤`

---

## Caption for the post (paste under the Reel)

Busy is not the same as growing.

Reinvention isn't a one-time event. It's a habit:
🧠 Mindset: stay curious, stay open, treat failure as data
📚 Learning: upskill, reskill, get the certification, build the side project
🎯 Impact: measure what changed, not what you did

What's one thing you've learned this year that changed a result, not just your to-do list? 👇

Save this for later and share it with someone who's ready for their next version.

#growthmindset #lifelonglearning #upskilling #reskilling #careergrowth #personaldevelopment #reinventyourself

## Filming tips (so the edit comes out clean)
1. Film vertically, with the phone at eye level, facing a window for light.
2. Record each section (1–6) as its own take. If you make a mistake, pause, then start that sentence again. I will keep the best take and cut the rest.
3. Leave about 1 second of silence before and after each section.
4. Use the phone's main (back) camera if you can. It gives a sharper image than the front camera.
5. Record in a quiet room. Soft furnishings (curtains, sofa) reduce echo.
